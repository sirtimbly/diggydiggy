Welcome to the dig.

A project is ground to explore. Some work is clear; some disappears into an unknown middle. Depth shows what comes next, without turning it into a deadline.

  • Explore the ground. Hold the middle mouse button and drag, or hold Space and drag with the left button, anywhere on the excavation. Release to return to moving blocks. You can pan across fresh ground sideways and downward, or use the mouse wheel to scroll the excavation. Space works normally while editing text.
  • Move the work. Drag a block to reorder the contact stack. The outline previews where it will snap, and nearby blocks make room. Bounds fit the colored box exactly, and edges snap together. Hold near a side or the bottom of the ground to keep scrolling into new columns or deeper; the world expands as needed. The reset-view control returns to the starting ground. With a block focused, left/right arrows change its column; up/down arrows change its order. Shift takes bigger steps.
  • Connect the work. Drag the lower link dot on a prerequisite onto the block that depends on it. Or select the dot, then select the dependent block. Arrows flow from prerequisite to dependent. Multiple prerequisites are supported; loops are rejected. Linked work always stays below its prerequisites, even while moving or resizing. Remove links in the inspector.
  • Follow the focus. Drag a digger onto a block, or select a digger and click a block. Each person can work in two places. Drag a placed digger to move that work site.
  • Reshape the work. Drag any edge or corner to resize a block. Focus a handle and use arrow keys to resize with the keyboard; Shift takes bigger steps. The solid stack snaps around the new size without adding padding. Select a block to adjust its uncertainty separately. The color fades out through pixel dithering, then returns at the bottom edge. Higher uncertainty hides more of the middle.
  • Make a little progress. Update progress deliberately. Diggers show activity with independent pickaxe rhythms, occasional coffee or phone pauses, and short walks along the exposed ledge. Edge-rooted, branching cracks show reported progress. Extracted blocks leave permanent cavities. Adjacent cavities join into a larger hole. Shafts above exposed stone are empty. Buried assignments wait until the work above is removed; diggers stand on exposed top faces. Depth is sequence, not time.

This is a local prototype with sample people and tasks. Changes stay in this browser. Shared live collaboration is a future step.
